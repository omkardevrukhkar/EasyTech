Emergency Numbers

On a phone? Just tap a number below to call right away.

In a life-threatening emergency, call 112 first. It connects you to police, fire and ambulance services anywhere in India.

Emergency & Rescue

Police, fire, ambulance and disaster response.

  • 112 Integrated Emergency Helpline (Police, Fire & Rescue, Health) Tap to call
  • 102 National Ambulance Service (NAS) Tap to call
  • 100 Police Helpline Tap to call
  • 101 Fire Helpline Tap to call
  • 1070 Relief Commissioner for Natural Calamities Tap to call
  • 011-2436-3260 Earthquake / Flood / Disaster (N.D.R.F.) Tap to call

Government & Consumer Helplines

Cyber crime, consumer issues, LPG safety and road accidents.

  • 1930 Cyber Crime Helpline Tap to call
  • 1915 National Consumer Helpline (NCH) Tap to call
  • 1933 National Narcotics Helpline (MANAS) Tap to call
  • 1906 LPG Leak Helpline Tap to call
  • 1073 Road Accident Helpline Tap to call

Senior Citizens & Persons with Disabilities

Support lines set up specifically for you.

  • 14567 Senior Citizens Helpline (also 011-2089-3999) Tap to call
