About Us

The increasing digitalization of healthcare and daily services presents both opportunities and challenges for senior citizens. Many older adults want to use smartphones for health and safety but find everyday apps confusing, text too small, or instructions written for people who already know the basics. This website project aims to bridge that digital divide by providing simple, accessible, and senior-friendly guidance on adopting technology — particularly smartphones — for health-related activities. It focuses on key areas such as how to operate a smartphone, how to connect to the internet (Wi-Fi and mobile data), and how to access basic healthcare services online (e.g., booking appointments, using telemedicine, and setting medicine reminders).

EasyTech was created to close that gap. It combines easy-to-understand tutorials, large fonts and adjustable text size, and a high-contrast mode, so reading and navigating the site never gets in the way of learning. The goal is simple: help senior citizens independently and confidently use their smartphones to improve their health, safety, and quality of life in a digital age.
